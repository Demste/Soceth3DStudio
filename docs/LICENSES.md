### Monetized video games

- **Commercial Game Use — Conditional** applies to native Pixal3D INT8/BF16 and TRELLIS.2 INT8/BF16. The reviewed chain permits use of generated assets in monetized games and their marketing, subject to the DINOv3 license and rights to the input images. DINOv3 grants worldwide use of its materials with trade-control and specified end-use restrictions. It has no revenue threshold or separate restriction on selling games containing generated assets. Research publications require acknowledgement. Redistributing DINOv3 files or derivatives requires its license, which is separate from shipping a game asset. Both TRELLIS.2 precisions are opt-in. [DINOv3 license](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3/blob/main/LICENSE.md) · [Pixal3D weights](https://huggingface.co/TencentARC/Pixal3D) · [TRELLIS.2 weights](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/TRELLIS.2)
- **Commercial Game Use — Reviewed** applies to the pinned TripoSR and TripoSG paths in Studio. The upstream code and model are MIT. TripoSG includes an Apache-2.0 DINOv2 encoder and uses the upstream hierarchical mesh path. The adapter accepts user-provided transparency and requires no BRIA weights or model service. TripoSR uses the official checkpoint, DINOv1 architecture configuration and BSD-licensed scikit-image surface extraction. TripoSR exports vertex color and TripoSG exports untextured geometry. [TripoSR](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR) · [TripoSR checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/TripoSR) · [TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG) · [TripoSG weights](https://huggingface.co/VAST-AI/TripoSG) · [DINOv2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/LICENSE) · [scikit-image](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/main/LICENSE.txt)
- **Commercial Game Use — Conditional** applies to Stable Fast 3D and SPAR3D. The relevant model license requires registration and limits the free commercial grant to eligible organizations below US$1 million in annual revenue. The gated Hugging Face pages require the account to accept model terms before obtaining files. Their Windows paths need working native texture-baker and UV-unwrapper extensions. SPAR3D also loads AlphaCLIP separately. These providers are outside the bundled catalog while the Windows extension build and auxiliary chain are unverified. [Stability license](https://stability.ai/license) · [Stable Fast 3D access](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-fast-3d) · [SPAR3D access](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-point-aware-3d)
- The first-party access sequence for those gated models is to register for commercial use with Stability, sign in on the relevant Hugging Face model page, accept its terms and authenticate the intended local environment with an account token that can read the gated repository. Studio does not fetch either gated package.
- **Commercial Game Use — Conditional** applies to Hunyuan3D 2.0, 2mv and 2.1. They are excluded from this catalog expansion because the Community Licenses restrict territories and the use or display of outputs outside those territories, including the EU, UK and South Korea.
